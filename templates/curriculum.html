Eight-week curriculum

Build the thinking you will need in a Salesforce interview.

Each week teaches a professional skill, gives a step-by-step study path, requires evidence, and connects the work to a real interview question.

01

Understand

Clarify the business problem, users, scope, constraints, and success criteria.

02

Research

Use credible evidence, compare options, and justify recommendations.

03

Design

Reason about data, security, automation, integration, quality, and delivery before building.

04

Present and defend

Explain the project clearly, answer follow-ups, and represent your experience honestly.
