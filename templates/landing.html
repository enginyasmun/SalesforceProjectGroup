Why this program exists

Learning Salesforce is not enough for an interview.

Bootcamp graduates often know objects, Flow, security, reports, Apex, and integrations, but struggle to explain what they actually did. The Project Group closes that gap.

The program does not misrepresent bootcamp work as employment. It teaches students to present project experience honestly, with professional depth and evidence.
